About Snake Game

Snake Game is a free online arcade game inspired by the classic snake games enjoyed around the world.

This game is made by William Kajuna from Kampala, Uganda.

Our goal is to provide a simple, enjoyable and accessible gaming experience on modern phones and computers.

Play, improve your skills and challenge your highest score!
